Important Notice Regarding Third-Party AI Processing (Google Gemini)
When using our Image to Prompt feature, images you submit are transmitted to Google's Generative Language (Gemini) API under Google's unpaid service tier. Under Google's Gemini API Additional Terms, Google may retain and use submitted content and machine-generated outputs to develop, improve, and train Google products and technologies, and trained human reviewers may view and process this content. Please do NOT upload personal, confidential, sensitive, or intimate photographs.

Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Odin Prompt is committed to protecting your privacy. We design our software on a privacy-first foundation: we do not collect your personal name, physical address, email address, contact list, location, microphone audio, or camera feed. Regular use of Odin Prompt does not require account registration.

1. Anonymous Firebase Authentication & Rate Limiting

When you launch Odin Prompt, the app signs in silently using Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This generates a random, pseudonymized identifier (UID). No personal details (such as email or name) are linked to this ID.

  • Purpose: To cryptographically authenticate your requests to our secure Cloudflare Worker proxy and enforce fair-use rate limits (1 image-to-prompt generation per rolling 24 hours for free users; 10 per day for VIPs).
  • Server Cache: Rate-limit counters are keyed solely to this random UID in temporary server cache (Cloudflare KV) with an automatic expiration time to delete after 48 hours. No user profile or personal information is saved in Firebase Firestore.

2. Image to Prompt & Google Gemini AI Processing (Third-Party Disclosure)

When you use the 'Image to Prompt' feature, the image you choose via the system Android Photo Picker is converted in memory on your device into a secure payload and transmitted via our Cloudflare Worker proxy directly to the Google Generative Language (Gemini) API to reverse-engineer aesthetic prompt descriptions (lighting, camera style, artist influence, keywords).

  • Google Gemini API Terms & AI Training: Our application utilizes Google's unpaid Gemini API quota. Under Google's Gemini API Additional Terms of Service, for unpaid services, Google may use submitted content (your uploaded images and generated prompts) and machine-generated responses to train, tune, and improve Google products, technologies, and services. Furthermore, trained human reviewers may read, annotate, and process your submitted content in a manner disconnected from our account and API keys.
  • Not Ephemeral on Google's Infrastructure: Processing is NOT ephemeral on Google's end; Google may retain and process submitted data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms.
  • Advisory Against Sensitive Photos: We strongly advise and warn users NOT to upload personal, private, sensitive, confidential, or intimate photos, or any images containing identifiable personal information.
  • Cloudflare Worker Proxy: Our Cloudflare Worker acts strictly as a secure relay (transiting data in memory over encrypted HTTPS) and does not store, archive, or log user images, photos, or prompts in databases, filesystems, or Cloudflare KV storage.

3. On-Device Local Data Storage

Your personal creative activity remains strictly on your device. The following items are stored exclusively in private local storage (Android SharedPreferences and local application directory):

  • Bookmarked prompts
  • Private Image-to-Prompt generation history and local thumbnail previews
  • 12-hour prompt unlock timers
  • VIP subscription toggle flag

This data is never transmitted to, uploaded to, or synchronized with external servers or cloud databases.

4. ImageKit Media Storage (Admin Catalog Only)

Odin Prompt utilizes ImageKit.io as a Content Delivery Network (CDN) solely to host sample inspiration artwork curated and uploaded by our administrators for the public prompt catalog. No end-user photos, images, or personal files are ever uploaded to or stored on ImageKit.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob) & User Consent (UMP / GDPR)

Free-tier users may choose to watch rewarded video ads to unlock curated prompts for 12 hours. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob.

  • Information Processed: Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, coarse network information, and app diagnostic logs to serve ads, prevent fraud, and report ad performance.
  • European Economic Area (EEA), UK & Switzerland: We implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (a certified Consent Management Platform complying with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework). Eligible users are prompted upon app launch to select their personalized or non-personalized ad preferences, which can be modified at any time via the "Ad Privacy & Consent Settings" button in the app's Privacy Policy screen.

6. Subscriptions & Billing Status

Currently, VIP status in Odin Prompt is provided as an on-device preview/test feature. Odin Prompt does not currently process credit card, debit card, or bank account payments, nor do we collect or store any financial data.

When official paid subscriptions are launched, all subscription purchases will be managed exclusively through Google Play In-App Billing and will be subject to Google Play's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy (Target Audience 18+)

Odin Prompt is intended strictly for users aged 18 years and older. The application is not directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe that an individual under 18 has used the application or provided any information, please contact us immediately at singharman57863@gmail.com so we can promptly delete such information.

8. User Rights & Data Deletion

Because your generation history, bookmarks, and thumbnails are stored solely on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual generation records or wipe your entire local history directly inside the Image to Prompt screen.
  • Complete App Reset: You can clear all data instantly by opening Android Settings > Apps > Odin Prompt > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Server-Side Rate-Limit Records: Temporary anonymous UID rate-limit counters automatically expire from server cache after 48 hours. You may contact singharman57863@gmail.com at any time to request immediate deletion of any server-side rate records.
  • Third-Party AI Data (Google): Photos and prompts transmitted to the Google Gemini API unpaid tier are retained and processed by Google under Google's Gemini Terms. Because Google disconnects this data from our application's account and API keys, Odin Prompt cannot directly view, retrieve, or delete data previously ingested by Google.

9. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security safeguards:

  • All network transmissions occur over encrypted HTTPS connections using TLS 1.3.
  • Requests to backend proxies are cryptographically authenticated using Firebase ID tokens.
  • User photos are never written to disk or long-term cloud databases on our servers.
  • Backend API keys and secrets are protected within server-side Cloudflare Workers and never exposed in client application binaries.
